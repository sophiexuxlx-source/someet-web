DATA PRIVACY

Privacy & Data Security

SoMeet is engineered with privacy at its core: speech is processed in volatile memory, session history remains securely in your browser, and voice recordings are never stored on our servers.

Zero Persistent Cloud Storage

Your raw speech audio, voice recordings, and transcribed text are never written to server databases. Authentication accounts maintain only basic quota and access records.

Private In-Browser Session

Transcripts and audio playback live entirely within your local browser memory. Closing or refreshing the tab clears temporary session media.

Enterprise-Grade AI Processing via Google Gemini

When you click "Generate Summary", only text transcripts (never raw audio files) are sent to the Google Gemini API under commercial enterprise terms, where data is not used for model training.

Hardware On-Demand Control

Microphone streaming is engaged strictly when you choose to record. You maintain transparent, absolute control over your audio hardware at all times.

SPEECH SETTINGS

Domain Context & Vocabulary

Provide specialized background context, industry terminology, or proper names to help the speech recognition engine transcribe with maximum precision.

Instant-Ready Microphone
