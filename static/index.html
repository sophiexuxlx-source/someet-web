PRIVACY

Privacy note

SoMeet has a narrow boundary: we do not store recordings or transcript text on our servers; realtime transcription is processed in memory; recent history stays in your browser.

No server-side content storage

We keep only the account access and quota records needed to run the service. We do not store recording files or transcript text on our servers.

Recent content stays in your browser

Transcripts and audio playback live temporarily in this browser session. During recording, the page keeps in-memory audio for continuous playback and export.

The Google Gemini API handles speech intelligence

Spoken text (never raw audio) is sent through our backend to the Google Gemini API when you click "Generate Summary". Google processes that data under its own Gemini API terms and privacy policy.

Microphone on-demand control

Microphone access is strictly activated when you click Record and immediately released when recording stops. Audio is never captured in the background.
